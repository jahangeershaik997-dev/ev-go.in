Privacy

What we collect. The details you type into a form: name, mobile number, area, vehicle or business details, language, and the time you agreed to be contacted.

Visit counts. When you open this page we record, without your name or number, a random visit code stored in your browser, which poster or link you came from, your phone type, your language and the time. We also note when you tap the WhatsApp or call links or start a form. We use this only to count visits and see which posters work. If you later send a form, we link it to the same visit code. Browsers that send a Do Not Track signal are not counted.

Why. Only to contact you about ev-go's launch and early sign-up. We do not sell your details.

Where it is kept. In a private Google Sheet that only the ev-go team can open. Google's services process it on our behalf.

How long. Until you ask us to delete it, or until we no longer need it to contact you about ev-go.

Your choices. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90003 14625 or call +91 97009 53452 to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop being contacted. You can do this at any time.
